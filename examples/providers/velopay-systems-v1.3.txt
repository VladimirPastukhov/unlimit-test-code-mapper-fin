VeloPay Systems — Error Code Reference (v1.3)

Every response carries a numeric `status_code` and a human-readable `status_text`.
Codes are grouped by the component that produced them.

=== Issuer Responses ===

1001 "Issuer declined"
    The issuing bank rejected the authorization request.
    No reason code was returned by the issuer.

1002 "Not enough funds"
    The cardholder's available balance is below the requested amount.

1003 "Card reported lost or stolen"
    The card has been reported lost or stolen by its owner.
    The issuer has permanently blocked it.

1004 "Card restricted"
    The issuer has placed a restriction on this card. Restrictions may be
    geographic, merchant-category based, or the result of issuer risk rules.
    The issuer does not say which one applies.

1005 "Exceeds withdrawal frequency"
    The cardholder has exceeded the number of transactions the issuer allows
    for this card in the current period.

1006 "Velocity check failed"
    The VeloPay risk engine blocked the payment after too many attempts from
    the same card or device within a short interval.

=== Gateway ===

2001 "Gateway error"
    An unhandled exception occurred inside the VeloPay gateway.

2002 "Acquirer unreachable"
    VeloPay could not open a connection to the acquiring bank.

2003 "Monthly volume cap reached"
    The merchant has processed the full volume agreed in its contract for
    the current calendar month.

2004 "Unknown status"
    The transaction outcome could not be determined. The payment may or may
    not have been captured; query the status endpoint before taking action.

=== Request Validation ===

3001 "Malformed request body"
    The request body is not valid JSON or does not match the API schema.

3002 "Invalid IBAN"
    The beneficiary IBAN has an invalid checksum or country prefix.

3003 "Amount below minimum"
    The amount is below the minimum allowed for the selected payment method.

=== Access Control ===

4001 "Missing Authorization header"
    The request did not include an Authorization header.

4002 "Merchant account suspended"
    The merchant account has been suspended by VeloPay compliance.
    Contact your account manager.

4003 "HMAC mismatch"
    The HMAC computed over the request body does not match the one supplied.

=== Shopper Flow ===

5001 "Shopper closed payment window"
    The shopper closed the hosted payment window before confirming.

5002 "Redirect not completed"
    The shopper was redirected to the bank authentication page but never
    returned. This happens when the shopper leaves, and also when the bank
    page fails to load.
